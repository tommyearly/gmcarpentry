# Image generation prompts

**Not used for launch.** The live site supplied enough genuine project photography for hero, stairs, en-suite, four case studies, gallery, carpentry, and videos.

If a future gap appears (for example a missing OG composition at 1200×630 that cannot be cropped from the hero), generate only a **layout frame** — logo + the real hero photograph + the line “There’s another room above you.” Never generate a fake Dublin attic, fake stairs, or fake customer.

Prompts reserved (Irish homes only, EU sockets, real roof geometry, no text, no workers smiling at camera):

1. Another room above — finished Dublin semi-d attic from the top of an integrated timber stair, two roof windows, warm daylight.
2. Dormer — accurate rear dormer on a Dublin tiled roof, interior headroom visible.
3. Attic en-suite — compact shower room under the slope, Velux, no luxury penthouse.
4. Work from above — modest home office under the ridge.
5. Staircase — painted spindles, matching existing landing.
6. Craft — close-up of a stair newel or built-in eaves cupboard.
